## Known limitations and biases

- Quality filtering is heuristic (length, repetition, symbol and URL ratios, boilerplate, punctuation); it favours well-formed prose and can remove legitimate lists, poetry, tables, or dialectal text.
- The built-in language detector covers a small set of languages and is weak on short or mixed-language text; non-target languages may be under-represented by design.
- Domain labels come from keyword and URL rules and inherit their blind spots.
- PII and secret detection is pattern based: it misses unstructured personal data and can flag look-alikes. Redaction placeholders (`<EMAIL>`, `<SECRET>`) remain in the text.
- Deduplication is document-level by default; near-duplicate detection is probabilistic (MinHash) with a Jaccard threshold, so lightly edited copies below the threshold remain.
- **This dataset was built from synthetic sample data** generated by DataForge for demonstration; its content is template-generated and not suitable for training real models.
